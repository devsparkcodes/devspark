# Course and Enrollment

## Current program
- Program: Generative AI Engineering
- Level: Level 01
- Batch: Cohort 01
- Duration: approximately 2–3 months as a working plan; exact duration TBD.
- Format: live online; schedule/platform details TBD.
- Age restriction: none defined.
- Enrollment: Google Form.
- First 1–2 classes: intended to be free so learners can experience the teaching before deciding whether to continue.

Use the public-facing naming **Generative AI Engineering — Level 01, Cohort 01** unless the user later changes it.

## Initial manual workflow
1. Student opens course page and selects Enroll Now.
2. Website opens the supplied Google Form.
3. Student enrolls and attends the announced free introductory classes.
4. Interested students decide whether to continue and pay.
5. Instructor privately shares payment account details.
6. Student sends payment proof by personal WhatsApp with name/email and requested details.
7. A separate spreadsheet tracks paid students.
8. Future class links go only to paid students.

No custom payment automation or student accounts in V1.

## Website constraints
Keep the Google Form URL in one central config/content location. Until supplied, use a clearly marked development placeholder and do not publish a broken link. Do not collect payment details on the website or expose a personal WhatsApp number unless explicitly approved.
