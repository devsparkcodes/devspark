## Confirmed course policies
- Late submissions (confirmed): no deadline extensions — a weekly assignment submitted after the Sunday 11:59 PM PKT deadline receives zero marks for that assignment. This rule applies to the six weekly assignments only; midterm and final project submission deadlines are announced in advance through the official course schedule and are not automatically covered by the weekly penalty.
- Score review (confirmed): a student may request a review of a published assignment, midterm or final-project score within 3 calendar days after the result is communicated, through the existing contact channels (contact page category "Course & enrollment question" or devspark.codes@gmail.com). DevSpark communicates the review decision within 5 calendar days of receiving a valid request. No appeals portal exists. Top-student winners are announced only after the review request window has closed and all valid review requests are resolved.
- Certificate eligibility (confirmed): both minimums required together — at least 80% attendance (20 of 24 sessions, the two free demos included in the denominator) and at least 60/100 overall — plus all six assignments and the midterm and final project submitted. Attendance exceptions are handled case by case and never guarantee a certificate.
- Top-three awards (confirmed): each of the top three eligible students receives a 100% refund of the fee actually paid (PKR 3,000 or PKR 4,000), settled by verified bank transfer within 7 calendar days after the final verified winners announcement (the countdown starts at that announcement, not at provisional results or the course end date); LinkedIn recognition only with explicit permission. Eligibility (strict): attend ALL 24 live sessions — both free demos and all 22 paid sessions — submit all six assignments, the midterm and the final project, and score at least 60/100. The certificate's case-by-case attendance exception does not grant award eligibility. Ranking: overall score out of 100; ties resolved by higher final-project score, then higher midterm score, then higher combined assignment score; a full tie shares a joint rank under standard competition ranking (1,1,3 / 1,2,2,4 / 1,2,3,3,5). All eligible students occupying competition ranks 1, 2 or 3 receive the award, so a complete tie can produce more than three recipients. Note for the owner: the combined-assignment tie-break is mathematically redundant once totals, final-project scores and midterm scores are all equal (total = assignments + midterm + final); the rule is preserved as approved — flagged, not changed.
- Paid enrollment (confirmed): paid enrollment and each early-bird place are confirmed only after DevSpark verifies the payment was actually received; a payment screenshot alone is not proof. Early-bird counting uses verified payments only; no live seat count is published. The interest form URL is `https://forms.gle/xpjhjQgRXKfnbJds9`.
- Student cancellation (confirmed): full refund of the fee actually paid if a cancellation request is received before 7:30 PM PKT on Friday, December 4, 2026 (the first paid class). The effective timestamp is when the request is received in the official inbox — devspark.codes@gmail.com — evaluated in Pakistan Standard Time; the request should identify the student and enrollment. The contact form, WhatsApp and the Google Form are NOT authorized cancellation channels. At or after that time, student-initiated cancellation is generally non-refundable, subject to applicable exceptions and legal requirements. A valid approved refund is processed within 14 calendar days of approval; the approval/verification turnaround is not yet independently specified. Do not collect banking credentials through public forms.
- If DevSpark cancels the course (confirmed): affected paid students receive a full refund of the fee actually paid within 14 calendar days of the official course cancellation notice. Separate from the seven-day award refund timeline.
- Significant course change (confirmed): the owner-selected qualifying change is **replacement of the lead instructor**. An affected paid student may request a 100% refund of the fee actually paid via devspark.codes@gmail.com; a valid approved request is refunded within 14 calendar days of approval. The request window and approval/turnaround are not yet specified — do not invent them. Ordinary schedule changes, minor timetable adjustments, tool changes and routine curriculum updates do NOT create this refund right. Preserve any rights or remedies required by applicable law.

## Registration workflow
1. Student opens the course page and selects **Register for Free Demo** (the single registration CTA).
2. Website opens the published Google Form.
3. Student submits the form (interest/free-demo participation); registered students attend the two free sessions.
4. Paid enrollment is allowed both before and after the free demos — a student may pay at any point, and DevSpark verifies each payment.
5. Instructor privately shares payment account details and confirms whether the student is within the first 20 confirmed paid enrollments (early-bird fee PKR 3,000, otherwise standard PKR 4,000).
6. Student sends payment proof by personal WhatsApp with name/email and requested details.
7. A separate spreadsheet tracks paid students — it is the current source of truth for payment-confirmed seats until the LMS exists.
8. Future class links (Google Meet) go only to paid students.

No custom payment automation or student accounts in V1. The Google Form never counts as a paid enrollment and never reserves early-bird pricing.
